The house房屋概览

Downstairs is one long room from the front door to the back lawn. Upstairs is where the family sleeps, and the master suite looks out to the city.一楼从正门到后院草坪一气呵成，宽敞通透；二楼是家人的卧室区，主卧套房可远眺城市景观。

The kitchen sits in the middle of it all: full-height walnut, a curved stone island and a butler's pantry behind, with the laundry next door. Dining and living open through wide glass doors to the covered alfresco and the pool.厨房位于整个空间的中心：通顶胡桃木橱柜、弧形石材岛台，后方设有备餐间，隔壁是洗衣房。餐厅与客厅通过宽大的玻璃门连接有盖户外休闲区和泳池。

Off the entry there's a media room and a fifth room that works as a gym, study or a bedroom for visiting family, with a shower room right beside it.入口旁设有影音室，另有一间可作健身房、书房或长辈来访时居住的客房，旁边紧邻带淋浴的卫生间。

Upstairs, four bedrooms surround a second living room. The master has two walk-in robes, a balcony facing the city and an ensuite with twin basins and a freestanding bath. A second bedroom has its own ensuite.二楼四间卧室环绕第二起居室。主卧配有两个步入式衣帽间、面向城市的阳台，以及双台盆和独立浴缸的套间浴室。另一间卧室也带独立浴室。
