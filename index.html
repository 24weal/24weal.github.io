The house

Downstairs is one long room from the front door to the back lawn, and upstairs is where the family lives.

The kitchen sits in the middle of it: full-height walnut, a curved stone island and a butler's pantry behind, with the laundry next door. Dining and living open through wide glass doors to a covered alfresco and the pool. Off the entry there's a media room and a fifth room that works as a gym, study or guest bedroom, with its own shower room beside it.

Upstairs, four bedrooms surround a second living room. The master has two walk-in robes, a balcony facing the city and an ensuite with twin basins and a freestanding bath. A second bedroom has its own ensuite, and the other two share the family bathroom.

It's built by Homes by CMA on a Truecore steel frame, which is termite-proof and carries a 50-year warranty. The external walls are AAC panel with R2.0 insulation. Ceilings are 3 metres downstairs, and the windows reach 2.4 metres. Final certificates were issued in August 2026, and the structural warranty and QBCC home warranty pass to the next owner.
